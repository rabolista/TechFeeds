# Privacy Policy for Tech and Feeds

Effective date: 2026-08-29

Tech and Feeds (bundle ID: com.ionicframework.techfeed247968) is a mobile app
that displays public technology news headlines and lets users open linked
articles.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is and is not collected when
you use the app.

## 1. Information We Collect

We do not require account registration.

We do not directly collect personal information such as:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Contacts
- Precise location
- Payment information

## 2. Information Processed by Third Parties

To function, the app loads public content from external services. When this
happens, those services may receive standard technical request data, such as:

- IP address
- Device and browser metadata
- Request timestamps
- Basic network diagnostics

This technical data is processed by the third-party service that receives
the request, according to that service's own privacy practices.

## 3. Third-Party Services and Links

The app may connect to the following services:

- hn.algolia.com (headline/content API)
- External websites opened from article links

When you open an external link, you leave the app context and are subject to
the privacy policy of that external site.

## 4. Data Use

The app uses network data only to:

- Retrieve and display news content
- Load related resources required for presentation

We do not sell personal information.

## 5. Data Retention

The app is designed as a lightweight reader and does not maintain user
accounts or user profile databases.

Saved articles and settings (such as theme and link-opening preference) are
stored locally on your device and can be cleared at any time from the
Settings tab.

## 6. Permissions

The app primarily requires network access to load content.

It is not designed to request sensitive runtime permissions for contacts,
microphone, camera, or precise location as part of core functionality.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The app is not specifically directed to children under 13.

## 8. Security

We use HTTPS endpoints where supported and limit network access to services
required for app functionality.

## 9. International Users

Because app content is delivered through internet-based services, data may
be processed in countries other than your own by those third-party
providers.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be
posted with a revised effective date.
